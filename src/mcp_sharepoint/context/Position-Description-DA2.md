Data Analyst II

Responsibilities:

Mastery of Data Analyst I responsibilities and performance
Independently proposes, designs and implements analytical and modeling features
Own complex projects from concept to implementation: scope, sequence and coordinate
work
Can-do attitude: able to navigate past roadblocks to achieve objectives
Provide stewardship and act as team expert for one or more of the team’s systems or
processes
Mentor and guide new analysts, engineers/scientists on the team
Focus on mastery of solutions to well-defined and well-understood problems
Pick up and become proficient in an analytics-applicable programming language (i.e. Python)
Exceptional Performance:

Completes single analyst project with no supervision
Completes single analyst projects in unfamiliar systems with little supervision
Substantially improves on the previously completed analysis in terms of scope, reach and/or
impact
Responsible for work repeatability: all analyses are well documented with comments
allowing someone not familiar with the work to get up to speed in a timely manner
Demonstrates leadership on team, removes obstacles to make others more effective
Proactively identifies, escalates and helps to resolve issues with existing systems
Works with Product/Customer Success/Operations team to review product specs and define
timelines
Respects engineering and analytical process and helps others to do the same
Sustains exceptional performance across multiple recent review periods (min. 2 years)
