Senior Data Analyst

Responsibilities:

Mastery of Data Analyst I and II responsibilities and performance
Acquire advanced understanding and ability to drive the analytical life cycle – from
idea/concept through spec requirements through analysis/model training followed by
analysis/model deployment
Lead the achievement of one or more of the team’s most important objectives
Improve the team’s code and/or analytics quality and experiment repeatability
Provide stewardship and act as team expert for several of the team’s most important
analyses, systems, or processes
Mentor and substantially contribute to the development of more junior team members on
the team
Strong communication skills: form strong relationships with partners across the business to
get things done
Work with the Product Team to review project specifications and schedule project work
Work with the Product Team to develop new, or enhance existing, products and
opportunities
Focus on mastery of solutions to partially-defined and partially-understood problems
Exceptional Performance:

Influences and leads analytical/technical direction of the team
Consistently helps team and manager/director succeed: provides tech leadership that
creates bandwidth for manager/director
Demonstrates leadership and a track record of delivering significant, business-impacting
projects on time with quality
Makes big, complex projects happen with no supervision
Facilitates effective and respectful product manager/analytics/engineering communication
Expert in all of the team's technology and tools
Positively influences team culture and morale; brings people together; finds and
promotes solutions when issues arise
Takes ownership of complicated cross-team issues and drives them to resolution
Expert in applicable technology and tools, provides technical mentorship for most of team's
members
Proactively identifies, escalates, and leads resolution of issues with most of the team's
systems
Navigates roadblocks and creates solutions that scale
Improves engineering process and ensures team's adherence to the same
Sustains exceptional performance across multiple recent review periods (min. 3 years)
