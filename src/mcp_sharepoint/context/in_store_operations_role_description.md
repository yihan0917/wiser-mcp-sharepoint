# In-Store Operations Role Descriptions

**DEPARTMENT: IN-STORE OPERATIONS**

*IMPORTANT: These role descriptions are ONLY for the In-Store Operations department. Do NOT apply these descriptions to similarly-titled roles in other departments (e.g., Engineering, Product, etc.). Each department has its own distinct role definitions and responsibilities.*

*Note: All job descriptions are relatively basic and intended to give an overview of what each role entails.*

---

## Retail Intelligence (RI)

### Data Validation Team

#### Sr. Data Quality Specialist / Data Quality Specialist

**Key Responsibilities:**
- **Validating Missions** - Validate result IDs in queue within Maya
- **Pinoffs Tasks** - Manage pinoff requests received through Help Scout
- **Data Quality Checks** - Verify data quality for accuracy of validated result IDs
- **Fraudulent Check** - Detect duplicate photo submissions across multiple result IDs and ensure submitted results are genuine and not fraudulent
- **Mission Guide Updates** - Update validator notes in mission guide to help validate result IDs for various missions
- **Re-validation** - Receive update requests from PMs/EMs for relevant missions, re-validate result IDs to maintain data quality and coverage, and ensure accuracy and completeness of data
- **ISPC Data Validation** - Validate ISPC data for errors and inconsistencies, update the portal with corrected and validated data to maintain accuracy
- **Product Tag Extraction** - Extract relevant information from product tag photos and update ISPC data with extracted details to complete product information

#### Sr. Team Lead / Team Lead

**Key Responsibilities:**
- **Mission Allocation** - Allocate missions to team prioritizing those highest on the priority list first
- **DQ Task Allocation** - Allocate missions for data quality tasks
- **Fraudulent User Check** - Extract users' data from Maya for fraudulent user verification based on results
- **Pinoffs Management** - Monitor and manage pinoffs tasks to ensure successful completion, address and resolve any queries or issues
- **Team Support** - Provide guidance and support to team members to ensure all tasks are efficiently completed while maintaining the highest data quality
- **Cross-functional Collaboration** - Work with PMs and EMs to address and resolve queries and issues, enabling the validation team to efficiently validate missions
- **Mission Reviews** - Participate in regular mission reviews with PMs and EMs to identify areas for improvement, reduce refusals, and increase coverage
- **MG Review** - Review mission guide drafts at the request of PMs, providing suggestions and feedback as needed
- **Ad Hoc Requests** - Handle ad hoc requests from PMs/EMs
- **Leave Coordination** - Coordinate with management to ensure leave requests align with business needs
- **Metrics Management** - Update monthly metrics
- **Recruitment** - Conduct interviews of new hires as and when required
- **Onboarding & Training** - Onboard and train new hires
- **Performance Management** - Conduct performance evaluation and management of the team
- **Documentation** - Create and maintain process and training documentation

## User Support

### User Support Associate

**Key Responsibilities:**
- **Email & Query Management** - Respond to general emails and queries from users in a timely and professional manner
- **User Inquiries** - Address user inquiries related to extra points, bonus points, mission issues, and other user concerns
- **Resolution Delivery** - Provide clear and concise resolutions to user queries, escalating complex issues to management as needed
- **Record Maintenance** - Maintain accurate records of user interactions and ensure all queries are resolved efficiently
- **Appeals Processing** - Review and process user appeals for refused result IDs in a fair and timely manner
- **Appeal Investigation** - Investigate each appeal by verifying user information and result ID details, assessing the reason for the initial refusal
- **Appeal Response** - Provide a clear and precise response to each appeal, including the outcome (approved or denied)
- **Tax Compliance** - Ensure user taxation compliance with government laws and regulations, implement and maintain accurate tax calculation and reporting systems
- **Social Media Monitoring** - Investigate user complaints and queries from Facebook groups that GTM (Go To Market) team reports
- **Metrics Tracking** - Maintain appeals metrics on a monthly basis

### Sr. Team Lead / Team Lead

**Key Responsibilities:**
- **Query Resolution** - Resolve user support associate queries for tickets requiring attention
- **Critical Issues** - Address critical issues raised by users with user support and deliver effective resolutions
- **Tax Monitoring** - Monitor user earnings for taxation purposes
- **Tax Form Follow-up** - Follow up with users to ensure timely submission of Form 9 and W-8BEN
- **Bonus Points** - Award bonus points to users who submit tax forms on time and notify them via email about their awarded points
- **Account Suspension** - Monitor users who have submitted tax forms on time and temporarily suspend accounts of those who have not, despite reminders
- **Metrics Management** - Update monthly metrics
- **eGifter Account Management** - Monitor the eGifter reward account to ensure a sufficient balance is maintained
- **Fund Coordination** - Coordinate with the respective department to request fund additions to the eGifter account
- **Gift Card Support** - Coordinate with eGifter support and users to resolve any gift card-related issues

## In-Store Price Check (ISPC)

### Data Collection Specialist, Contractor

**Key Responsibilities:**
- **Data Collection** - Hired contractor responsible for gathering UPCs (Universal Product Code), prices, and other relevant information for assigned checklists using our company's mobile Price Check application

### Technical Implementation Specialist

**Key Responsibilities:**
- **Script Execution** - Run all customer deliverable scripts and ensure they are delivered to customers as needed and on time
- **Data Tickets** - Perform weekly data tickets for in-store price checks related to database management or other needs at the customer's request that can't be easily extracted through an Admin interface

### Sr. Technical Operations Analyst

**Key Responsibilities:**
- **SQL Analysis** - Utilize SQL to derive information from our price check database for assigned data tickets that can't be easily extracted through an Admin interface
- **Scrape Management** - Schedule and oversee all customer scrapes, ensuring that data imports are successful and meet both customer and operational needs. Address any issues by collaborating closely with the Operations Manager, online scrape team, and Engineering teams to investigate and resolve them

### Operations Manager

**Key Responsibilities:**
- **Quality Assurance** - Oversee the execution, quality assurance, and maintenance of In-Store Price Checks to meet goals and ensure timely deliverables for customers
- **Coverage Management** - Maintain coverage to fulfill price check requirements, effectively managing remote contractors to meet deliverable due dates
- **Cross-functional Collaboration** - Collaborate cross-functionally with the Online Scrape Team, Engineering, Validation Team, Customer Success, and Data Analytics Team to address all Price Check-related needs, ensure customer standards are met, and drive process improvements
- **Cost & Metrics Management** - Monitor and manage all costs and data metrics to meet monthly COGS and data accuracy goals
- **Issue Resolution** - Handle customer questions, manage contractor issues, and address new requests related to price checks
- **Contractor Management** - Oversee communication and scheduling for our field of Price Collector contractors, handle hiring, training, and contractor payment, and ensure effective team performance and coverage
