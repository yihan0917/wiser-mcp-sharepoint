# Data Management Career Path Guidance

This document outlines the career progression for data management professionals, detailing expectations across technical skills, delivery capabilities, and teamwork/collaboration/leadership qualities at each level. At L5 and above, there are three career tracks: Mastery (Individual Contributor), Architect, and Management.

---

## L1 - Associate Data Engineer

### Technical
- Able to code in at least one of the team's technology stacks and languages
- Can write basic SQL to query data
- Can independently debug and fix simple bugs
- Can work independently on simple features
- Proactively seeks help when working on more complex stories
- Follows the team's engineering best practices

### Delivery
- Follows the team's Agile practices
- Elicits feedback within PRs from more senior team members and applies the requested changes appropriately
- Can be counted on to deliver on their commitments, proactively seeking assistance as needed
- Delivers high quality, clean, easy to understand code which is properly tested

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership
- Seeks out and incorporates feedback to improve skills
- Effectively utilizes more senior team members for help and guidance

---

## L2 - Data Engineer

### Technical
- Can write queries against different data solutions (e.g. Redshift, Snowflake, Mongo, etc.)
- Works on well defined stories with minimal assistance
- Understands and follows design patterns established in the code base
- Understands and follows the team's engineering best practices
- Understands and applies basic index strategies and query optimization techniques
- Can understand an Entity-Relation Diagram (ERD) and can translate that into a physical schema design

### Delivery
- Solid contributor in at least one area of the codebase
- Understands and follows the team's Agile practices
- Can be counted on to deliver on their commitments with minimal assistance
- Able to understand edge cases with data and writes unit tests to validate against them

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership
- Seeks out and incorporates feedback to improve skills
- Effectively utilizes more senior team members for help and guidance

---

## L3 - Data Engineer II

### Technical
- Is able to utilize previous experience to quickly learn and become productive in new tech stacks
- Can design small areas with assistance from more senior engineers
- Proactively identifies opportunities to improve code health
- Understands the differences between OLTP and OLAP systems and makes improvements against each
- Is able to build components of a data pipelines that effectively processes Big Data with some guidance
- Contributes to the design of new capabilities
- Successfully demonstrates how best to work with different data formats (columnar, row, document, etc.)
- Demonstrates effective database administration practices
- Understands database performance metrics and create threshold alerts for them
- Understands Big O notation and applies it to query optimization/data partition strategies

### Delivery
- Works independently on well defined stories
- Can be counted on to independently deliver on their commitments
- Ensures that changes made to the data pipeline have adequate unit tests in place
- Follows Infrastructure guidelines and best practices to create and manage Data Platform components

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership
- Is able to balance helping others on the team with delivering on own commitments
- Collaborates with other Engineering teams to support and implement Data Management solutions

---

## L4 - Senior Data Engineer

### Technical
- Is a subject matter expert in at least one area of the code
- Is truly T-shaped and can contribute to multiple areas of the codebase
- Can solve complex problems and implement critical functionality
- Easily picks up new tech stacks and can quickly become productive in unfamiliar code
- Owns the technical design of a feature and is able to defend it within the team
- Promotes coding best practices on the team and provides valuable feedback in PRs
- Makes major contributions to the definition of the team's tech debt backlog
- Is able to evaluate different data technologies and understands the pros/cons
- Provides suggestions on data technologies/frameworks to leverage to solve specific use cases
- Proactively monitors data pipelines for bottlenecks and works to optimize them
- Is able to "right-size" the infrastructure that supports the Data Platform
- Demonstrates an understanding on the nuance differences in business rules between systems and how it can affect the data

### Delivery
- Is able to work completely independently on an initiative and consistently delivers business value
- Clarifies requirements, thinks through edge cases and identifies missed requirements
- Understands the business goal behind the stories the team is asked to do and when appropriate proposes alternate technical solutions to achieve business goal
- Collaborates with product and business stakeholders to ensure features meet business needs
- Coordinates dependencies within the team to deliver on features
- Understands how the team is tracking against their delivery goals and suggests ways to course correct when the team falls behind
- Promotes strong quality practices across the team
- Has the forethought to assist other team members to ensure overall delivery can be met

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership
- Guides and mentors more junior members of the team helping them grow their skills
- Supports other team members, in addition to doing own tasks in order to achieve the commitment
- Actively contributes to the team's continuous improvement by suggesting ways for the team to improve execution
- Provides guidance to other Engineering teams on best practices of using Data Management solutions

## L5 - Lead Data Engineer / Data Architect / Data Engineering Manager

**At L5, there are three career tracks:**
- **Mastery Track:** Lead Data Engineer (Individual Contributor)
- **Architect Track:** Data Architect
- **Management Track:** Data Engineering Manager

### Technical (All Tracks)
- Has expert-level knowledge of the systems developed by the team
- Has a deep understanding of the domain of their product area enabling the design of technical solutions that meet business needs
- Promotes a culture of designing maintainable, well tested and future resilient solutions
- Can be counted on to tackle the team's most complex technical problems
- Understands fundamentals of Data Governance and how to adopt practices supporting it
- Identifies the direct costs and opportunity costs associated with technology choices
- Understands security/privacy implications of the data architecture and can implement effective controls
- Can conduct a gap analysis between different systems to identify the business rule variances that would affect the data
- Actively improves Data Democratization, Data Stewardship, and Data Integration capabilities within Wiser

### Technical (Mastery Track - Lead Data Engineer)
- Strong contributor across multiple areas of the codebase
- Go to person on the team for multiple areas of the codebase that the team owns
- Establishes best practices in multiple disciplines for the team
- Provides strategies to maintain consistent uptime of the Data Platform components that the team manages

### Technical (Architect Track - Data Architect)
- Stays abreast of new technologies and identifies ways to appropriately utilize these technologies in their product area
- Identifies areas of architectural debt in their product area and suggests projects to improve quality, stability and velocity
- Owns the technical design of multiple features/capabilities and is able to defend it with other Engineering teams
- Understands "Data UX" with respect to the "Data Spectrum" and applies it to building data products that other stakeholders consume

### Technical (Management Track - Data Engineering Manager)
- Is able to take a high level vision and distill it down into actionable plan that the team can execute on
- Owns, contributes to, and prioritizes the team's tech debt backlog
- See Manager Track Role Description for Data Engineering Manager for additional responsibilities

### Delivery (All Tracks)
- Is able to prioritize/sequence tasks that builds momentum in achieving the targeted goals
- Drives strong quality practices across the team

### Delivery (Mastery Track)
- Ensures the successful deployments of feature/capability releases

### Delivery (Architect Track)
- Suggests new tools and processes that can help improve team execution
- Assists in the curation of the Data Platform product roadmap

### Delivery (Management Track)
- Helps drive the Agile process of the team
- Takes responsibility for the team's release commitments, understands how the team is tracking against those commitments, and rallies others to course correct when the team falls behind
- Coordinates dependencies with other teams to enable team to make forward progress on work
- Proactively communicates with other teams as to the progress made against team's initiatives
- See Manager Track Role Description for Data Engineering Manager for additional responsibilities

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership (All Tracks)
- Promotes cross-training and elimination of skill silos on the team through formal and informal knowledge sharing
- Promotes cross-functional collaboration with product and business stakeholders to ensure team has the proper business context and is building the right features
- Promotes a culture of continuous improvement by suggesting ways for the team to improve execution
- Evangelizes Wiser Engineering guidelines and guardrails with team
- Collaborates with other Engineering teams to establish the right execution plan that incorporates Data Management best practices
- Collaborates with other teams in identifying ways to improve on their relevant data quality KPIs
- Provides guidance to other teams regarding data management best practices

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership (Mastery Track)
- Shares expertise across the Engineering organization

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership (Architect Track)
- Works with Architecture and Product to define the long term roadmap for the product area
- Ensures teams understand and align with the long term architectural vision
- Provides guidelines to other Data Team members in support of "Data UX" and the "Data Spectrum" concepts

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership (Management Track)
- Proactively helps team members meet commitments by identifying and eliminating blockers and slowdowns
- Promotes a workplace culture that builds camaraderie amongst team members
- Understands team member's career aspirations and is able to define a pathway to help them meet it
- See Manager Track Role Description for Data Engineering Manager for additional responsibilities

## L6 - Principal Data Engineer / Data Architect II / Senior Engineering Manager

**At L6, there are three career tracks:**
- **Mastery Track:** Principal Data Engineer (Individual Contributor)
- **Architect Track:** Data Architect II
- **Management Track:** Senior Engineering Manager

### Technical (All Tracks)
- Leads and contributes to complex technical initiatives that span multiple teams
- Has a deep understanding of the business domain of their product family enabling the design of technical solutions that meet business needs
- Drives initiatives in support of defined Data Governance principles
- Leverages existing data to build new data products that helps enrich our intellectual property
- Proactively makes improvements that reduce costs and improves productivity for Data Stakeholders

### Technical (Mastery Track - Principal Data Engineer)
- Has expert-level knowledge of systems within the product family
- Owns the evolutionary design of multiple capabilities within the Data Platform stack
- Proactively identifies and implements solutions that improve the efficiency of Data Stakeholders
- Owns and manages the uptime of the various Data Platform components
- Designs and builds solutions that enable other teams to "self-service" Data Platform components

### Technical (Architect Track - Data Architect II)
- Owns and manages the Data Platform roadmap with respect to Data Democratization, Data Stewardship, and Data Integration
- Understands the future evolution of data management paradigms and incorporates the principles into the roadmap for the Data Platform

### Technical (Management Track - Senior Engineering Manager)
- Balances the execution of the Data Platform backlog in conjunction with other teams' backlogs
- See Manager Track Role Description for Senior Data Engineering Manager for additional responsibilities

### Delivery (All Tracks)
- Facilitates cross-team collaboration to achieve shared outcomes
- Identifies and remediates systemic issues that slow teams down
- Promotes a culture of continuous improvement across multiple teams by suggesting ways to improve execution
- Champions strong quality practices across multiple teams

### Delivery (Mastery Track)
- Owns the quality of the data produced/managed by the Data Platform components

### Delivery (Architect Track)
- Suggests new tools and processes that can help improve team execution
- Provides a strategy of evolving the architecture/integrations with minimal disruption to external stakeholders

### Delivery (Management Track)
- Ensures initiatives are delivered predictably
- Takes responsibility for the release commitments across the product family, understands how the teams are tracking against those commitments and rallies others to course correct when the teams falls behind
- See Manager Track Role Description for Senior Data Engineering Manager for additional responsibilities

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership (All Tracks)
- Actively assists in the improvement of data quality across the company
- Takes ownership in improving the Data Management practices of other teams
- Contributes to refining the Wiser Engineering guidelines and guardrails and evangelizes their use across multiple teams
- Evangelizes Data Governance principles with other stakeholders and provides guidance on execution
- Promotes a culture of self reliance and "owning your own destiny" within the team

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership (Mastery Track)
- Shares expertise across the Engineering organization
- Educates teams on new technologies, design patterns, coding best practices and testing best practices

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership (Architect Track)
- Works with Architecture and Product to define the long term architectural roadmap for their area to support current and future business needs
- Collaborates with senior staff in other business units to build common components and practices that benefit the entire organization
- Evangelizes "Data UX" and the "Data Spectrum" concepts within the Engineering team

### Teamwork, Collaboration, and Leadership (Management Track)
- Proactively helps team members meet commitments by identifying and eliminating blockers and slowdowns
- Promotes a workplace culture that builds camaraderie across different teams
- See Manager Track Role Description for Senior Data Engineering Manager for additional responsibilities
