# Engineering Leadership Career Path Guidance

This document contains the role descriptions for the people leadership track of the Engineering career ladder.

**Leadership Levels:**
- Engineering Manager
- Senior Engineering Manager  
- Director of Engineering
- Senior Director of Engineering
- Vice President of Engineering
- Senior Vice President / Chief Technology Officer

---

## Engineering Manager

The Engineering Manager is the first role on the people leadership track. This is a contributing manager position. Engineering Managers are responsible as the technical lead of a team and for hands-on delivery of work. They are considered an expert in the business domain of their product line. They are strong communicators, role models and drivers of agile software delivery best practices. Engineering Managers are seen as contributors actively engaging in Engineering department activities beyond the scope of their team.

**Prerequisites:** Everything for Senior Engineer, plus the following...

### Technical Excellence
- Demonstrates proficiency in engineering best practices
- Evangelizes Wiser Engineering guidelines and guardrails with team
- Has expert-level knowledge of systems developed by team
- Ensures appropriate technical discovery and design is done before implementation
- Presents multiple solution options with clearly articulated tradeoffs – finds ways to be successful and meet both the business and technical needs
- Participates actively in the delivery process with hands-on contribution of high-quality code
- Collaborates and partners effectively with Architecture, Data Engineering, Data Science, and Infrastructure to deliver resilient solutions that align with our long-term technical vision
- Builds strong relationships with other engineering leaders
- Actively engaged in one or more department initiatives such as guilds, special interest groups, TAFs

### Process Excellence
- Acts as agile coach for team, promoting good practice discipline
- Fosters a continuous improvement mindset
- Challenges team to proactively identify issues and find ways to improve delivery
- Regularly tracks and shares key metrics on team performance
- Facilitates effective team meetings
- Collaborates and partners effectively with Product and UX leads to set goals, discover, and deliver value for our customers and business stakeholders
- Creates delivery forecasts and provides regular updates on progress to business stakeholders
- Accountable to achievement of the team's commitments, challenges the team to meet their goals when they fall behind
- Accountable for the systems the team owns from problem discovery all the way through to production delivery and operation
- Leads definition of team on call procedures, communicates effectively with leadership about production issues, leads RCAs & follow ups for serious production issues
- Sets a high-quality bar for the team's work product

### People Management
- Ensures team is properly skilled to achieve their mission
- Proactively sets personal goals with and mentors team members 1:1 without prompting
- Advocates for work assignments and training opportunities to grow the careers of team members
- Seeks opportunities to train team members to create redundancy and backfill ability
- Delivers ongoing performance feedback and writes/delivers performance reviews
- Proactively addresses performance issues on the team
- Promotes a healthy team culture, motivating team to stretch and achieve, appropriately recognizing and rewarding team members for strong contributions

## Senior Engineering Manager

The Senior Engineering Manager often has a scope of responsibility and influence beyond a single delivery team. They are considered force multipliers that make the entire engineering organization better. Senior Engineering Managers have business expertise extending beyond a single product line. They may manage multiple teams. They contribute to the development of people and process best practices and influence other managers to improve their practices.

**Prerequisites:** Everything for Engineering Manager, plus the following...

### Technical Excellence
- Instills engineering best practices in team members
- Evangelizes Wiser Engineering guidelines and guardrails across the department
- Builds expert-level knowledge of team-owned systems in managers and leads
- Reviews and improves the technical discovery and design process
- Helps managers and leads identify multiple solution options with clearly articulated tradeoffs
- Guides delivery leads to ensure their designs fit the long-term business and technical vision
- Actively contributes to the refinement and extension of Wiser Engineering guidelines and guardrails
- Builds strong relationships with other engineering leaders
- Leads cross-team initiatives such as guilds or special interest groups

### Process Excellence
- Effectively leads & balances priorities across multiple delivery teams (managing Managers)
- Ensures teams have a clearly established charter, mission, norms, and expectations of each other
- Develops agile coaching skills in team managers
- Guides teams to set ambitious but realistic goals
- Works with Product leaders to ensure effective collaboration is happening between engineering managers, product managers, and UX leads
- Fosters a continuous improvement mindset
- Establishes metrics for tracking team delivery and effectiveness
- Leads initiatives that improve delivery practices within Engineering
- Effectively tracks, mitigates, and communicates about risks and issues
- Collaborates and partners effectively with Product and UX leads to set goals, discover, and deliver value for our customers and business stakeholders
- Effectively navigates through ambiguity and tackles complex issues requiring negotiation with multiple stakeholders
- Fosters a strong sense of accountability, ownership and urgency
- Acts as quality champion, owning the quality strategy and setting a very high standard for teams

### People Management
- Ensures manager review feedback is thoughtful, balanced and constructive
- Coaches senior team members to become leaders
- Brings solutions to problems and coaches others to do the same
- Collaborates effectively with HR team to improve recruiting, drives team leads to good hiring practices

### Strategy
- Seeks to understand technical challenges of other teams and what they do to identify solutions that benefit multiple groups

## Director of Engineering

The Director of Engineering is a deep technical expert in a software engineering discipline and/or a product portfolio. Directors are responsible for the staffing, planning and budget management for their portfolio. Directors of larger portfolios will manage multiple Engineering Managers. They are cross-disciplined proactive leaders that solve our most difficult business and technical problems.

**Prerequisites:** Everything for Senior Engineering Manager, plus the following...

### Technical Excellence
- Contributes extensively to the technical vision and is a champion of this vision for the entire Engineering organization
- Owns the development of Wiser Engineering best practices, guidelines, and guardrails
- Develops the technical vision (aligned tightly with the enterprise technical vision), technical roadmap (aligned tightly with the product roadmap) and technical strategy for their discipline / portfolio
- Demonstrates a deep understanding of the technical vision, technical roadmap and technical strategy of peer Engineering disciplines
- Very strong relationship builder with other engineering leaders

### Process Excellence
- Effectively leads an entire discipline or product portfolio
- Effectively partners with product leadership to develop the roadmap, goals, and delivery plans
- Responsible for properly staffing and budgeting their teams to deliver on the portfolio business strategy and customer need
- Has a track record for their teams' successfully planning, executing and delivering results for the business
- Contributes directly to cost benefit analysis for potential new initiatives
- Leads initiatives that improve delivery practices within Engineering
- Represents Engineering effectively in executive and stakeholder meetings

### People Management
- Drives consistent improvement of people management and process within the department
- Ensures every member of their team understands the portfolio business strategy and customer need
- Guide engineering managers to create career development plans for their direct reports
- Ensure engineering managers are effectively mentoring and managing their teams
- Be an exemplary model for optimism, identifying solutions for complex problems and coaching others to do the same
- Build a team culture that embraces experimentation and appropriately rewards strategic failures as well as successes

### Strategy
- Effectively partners with other discipline leads to solve large cross-functional technical challenges
- Understands business needs and strategy of other portfolios
- Works directly with leaders in other departments to understand their needs and to help them understand how the Engineering team is working towards those needs
- Able to clearly articulate the importance of their teams' technical work to non-technical audiences

## Senior Director of Engineering

The Senior Director of Engineering leads a software engineering discipline and/or a product portfolio. Senior Directors are strategic contributors across the company. They identify, drive and deliver on material new opportunities for the company. Senior Directors have a clearly articulated vision, roadmap and strategy for their portfolio. They are clearly recognized by other company leaders as strategic partners.

**Prerequisites:** Everything for Director of Engineering, plus the following...

### Technical Excellence
- Owner of the enterprise technology vision & strategy – driving alignment across the disciplines and product portfolios
- Communicates a clear technical vision, roadmap and strategy that is well understood across the company
- Frequently leads strategic technical discussions and decision making with peers

### Process Excellence
- Effectively leads an entire discipline or product portfolio
- Demonstrated record of building teams focused on and successful at delivering results while constantly improving process
- Develops and implements process and procedures that are innovative, efficient and position the company for long term success
- Seen as a process champion across the department

### People Management
- Develops resourcing process that strategically plans for the future strategies, priorities and needs of the business
- Partners effectively with peers within both Engineering and other departments to build consensus and drive forward big decisions
- Effectively partners with other discipline leads to solve large cross-functional technical challenges
- Actively coaches and drives growth of other senior leaders within Engineering
- Provides a good balance of direction and empowerment so leaders and teams are making decisions that are aligned with the company, product and technical vision

### Strategy
- Deep understanding of entire Wiser business and the strategy and needs in each area
- Actively identifies new opportunities to leverage solutions for the company, gains product and business support for these initiatives, and champions them to successful completion
- Is a leader within the company leadership team and takes ownership of cross-department leadership team initiatives
- Effectively communicates ideas to the executive team
- Seen as a strong strategic partner and influencer by leaders in other departments

---

## Vice President of Engineering

The Vice President of Engineering will typically manage multiple Directors, each of which leads a discipline or product portfolio. VPs develop other senior leaders within the company and contribute meaningfully to the overall company strategy. VPs demonstrate a broad impact across the entire company. They improve the effectiveness of the engineering organization and work with the CTO to develop the long-term growth plan of the engineering organization. VPs are outward facing to prospects and customers.

**Prerequisites:** Everything for Senior Director of Engineering, plus the following...

### Technical Excellence
- Owner of the enterprise technology vision & strategy – driving alignment across the disciplines and product portfolios
- Communicates a clear technical vision, roadmap and strategy that is well understood across the company

### Process Excellence
- Effectively leads multiple disciplines or product portfolios
- Continuously improves the team's capacity and capability to deliver by monitoring performance and execution
- Proactively identifies department inefficiencies and blockers, working with directors and other leaders to eliminate them
- Focuses on ensuring that all engineering effort is aligned with achieving company OKRs as fast and effectively as possible
- Seen as a process champion across the company

### People Management
- Pushes people to make (and own) tough and unpopular decisions when needed to make an impact on the business
- Mentors and coaches senior leaders across the company, acts as a sponsor for leadership development
- Drives a results-first culture of delivery, transparency and ownership
- Ensures directors are mentoring, managing and directing their teams to effectively hitting their goals
- Actively coaches and drives growth of other senior leaders across the company
- Provides a good balance of direction and empowerment so leaders and teams are making decisions that are aligned with the company, product and technical vision

### Strategy
- Seen as a strong strategic company leader by leaders in other departments
- Works closely with company leadership to contribute to company strategy, vision and goals
- Partners with CTO to develop and improve the engineering team's overall structure, organization and delivery of results
- Effectively communicates to executive team, investors, board members, and other company partners
- Frequently speaks to other teams within the company on engineering-related topics
- Acts as an executive sponsor for customers (when needed)

## Senior Vice President / Chief Technology Officer

The CTO is the executive responsible for the technical foundation of the company. This person is accountable for the performance of the entire development organization. The CTO collaborates with other company executives to set corporate strategic direction. This person sets culture, direction, and strategy for the Engineering organization. The CTO is outward facing to the rest of the company, customers, and the industry.

**Prerequisites:** Everything for Vice President of Engineering, plus the following...

### Technical Excellence
- Aligns the technical vision and strategy with the company vision and strategy
- Making recommendations and present business advantages for technology initiatives and improvements
- Sponsors initiatives that drive forward the technical vision

### Process Excellence
- Effectively leads the Engineering department
- Creates and leads a healthy, collaborative and results-focused department leadership team
- Works with CEO and CFO to establish overall department budget, manages budget effectively throughout the year
- Establishes overall Engineering org structure, growth plans, strategy and practices

### People Management
- Sets, owns and drives overall Engineering culture
- Mentors and coaches senior leaders across the company, acts as a sponsor for leadership development
- Drives a results-first culture of delivery, transparency and ownership
- Ensures directors & VPs are mentoring, managing and directing their teams to effectively hitting their goals
- Actively coaches and drives growth of other senior leaders across the company

### Strategy
- Works with CEO and executive team to establish company mission, vision, values, and goals
- Effectively communicates company vision and strategy across the company
- Acts as the public face of Wiser Engineering organization
- Seen as a strong strategic company leader inside and outside of Wiser
- Effectively communicates to executive team, investors, board members, and other company partners
- Acts as an executive sponsor for customers (when needed)
