Analytics Manager
The Analytics Manager is the first role on the people leadership track. This is a contributing manager position.
Analytics Manager is responsible for technically leading of a team and for hands-on delivery of work. He is
considered an expert in the business domain of his product line. He is a strong communicator, role model and
driver of agile analytics delivery best practices.

Technical Excellence

Mastery of I, II, Senior levels of responsibilities with a significant emphasis on analytical and modeling
skills.
Proficiency in analytical theory and application (descriptive, predictive, prescriptive analytics) with the
ability to serve as a subject matter area expert for the functional area of responsibility and the greater
Data Science team
Demonstrates proficiency in analytics best practices
Evangelizes Wiser Data Science Engineering guidelines and guardrails with team
Leads teams and determines the roadmap of future work in detail (what, how, who)
Has expert-level knowledge of systems developed and/or utilized by the team
Ensures appropriate analytical and technical discovery and design is done before implementation
Presents multiple solution options with clearly articulated tradeoffs – finds ways to be successful and
meet both the business and technical needs
Participates actively in the delivery process with hands-on contribution of high-quality analytical code.
Promotes code reviews of analytical findings
Collaborates and partners effectively with Architecture, Data Engineering, Data Science, and
Infrastructure to deliver resilient solutions that align with our long-term technical vision
Builds strong relationships with other leaders in the company
Process Excellence

Owns the output of the team and every aspect of the team function:
o Creating documentation
o Division of work
o Assigning the right person to the right task
o Successful and fast onboarding
o Communication with stakeholders
o Systems performance
o Continuation of value delivery to customers
Builds and documents processes and protects the team from black swan events (“hit by the bus”/”won
the lottery”). The documentation created is complete, exhaustive, and optimal and allows the team to
focus on delivering results fast with quality and not resolving process/people issues
Acts as agile coach for team, promoting good practice discipline
Fosters a continuous improvement mindset
Challenges team to proactively identify issues and find ways to improve delivery
Regularly tracks and shares key metrics on team performance
Facilitates effective team meetings
Collaborates and partners effectively with Sales, Product, UX and Engineering leads to set goals,
discover, and deliver value for our customers and business stakeholders
Creates delivery forecasts and provides regular updates on progress to business stakeholders
Accountable to achievement of the team’s commitments, challenges the team to meet their goals
when they fall behind
Accountable for the systems the team owns from problem discovery all the way through to production
delivery and operation
Leads definition of team on call procedures, communicates effectively with leadership about
production issues, leads RCAs & follow ups for serious production issues
Sets a high-quality bar for the team’s work product
People Management

Ensures team is properly skilled to achieve their mission
Proactively sets personal goals with and mentors team members 1:1 without prompting
Advocates for work assignments and training opportunities to grow the careers of team members
Seeks opportunities to train team members to create redundancy and backfill ability
Delivers ongoing performance feedback and writes/delivers performance reviews
Proactively addresses performance issues on the team
Promotes a healthy team culture, motivating team to stretch and achieve, appropriately recognizing
and rewarding team members for strong contributions
