# Engineering Overview

## Summary

With Wiser's organic and acquisition growth, we have brought together many teams with different cultures. It's vital to our success to create a unified culture and strategy so that we can work effectively together to achieve our company mission and goals.

## Why This Matters

This is important for team members to understand so that we are always building toward the technical vision needed to achieve the company vision, we are focusing our efforts in the right areas, and we are all aligned on the same approach to building solutions. The latter is important so that teams can work independently but cohesively towards shared goals and that we can deliver the most value together per effort invested.

## How to Use This Information

Use this section to better understand our expectations for delivering solutions for Wiser and what you need to be thinking about as you design, build and maintain these solutions. Ask questions if the solution your team is working on does not align with our principles, fit our strategy, or you see behavior out of line with our values.

## Engineering's Why (Mission & Vision)
The Engineering mission statement supports and enables our company mission. We are here to be the first to build a commerce execution platform.

Building that platform and delivering the solutions on top of it for our customers needs to be done with high quality given the high level of trust our customers need to place on our solutions to power their business decisions. Our approach also needs to enable us to deliver value quickly and to build systems that can easily integrate acquired businesses (a key part of our growth strategy).

### Benefits of Evolutionary Architecture

- **Flexibility and Adaptability** - Systems can quickly adapt to new requirements, technologies, or changes in the business environment

- **Reduced Risk** - Incremental changes allow for more manageable risk as adjustments are made in controlled, small increments

- **Sustainability** - The architecture can endure and remain effective over the long term as it evolves in response to emerging challenges and opportunities

- **Continuous Improvement** - Regular updates and improvements based on ongoing feedback loops help ensure that the system remains aligned with business needs and technology trends

### Implementation

To implement an evolutionary architecture, we will adopt practices like continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD), which facilitate frequent updates. We will rely heavily on automation, such as automated contract testing, to maintain stability and ensure that changes do not break existing functionality. Our overall goal is to create a system that can continually evolve without requiring extensive rework or becoming obsolete, thereby supporting the long-term growth and changing needs of the organization.

Evolutionary architecture is particularly beneficial in environments like ours where business requirements change frequently or where innovation is critical to success. It will allow us to be more responsive and agile, hopefully turning the ability to adapt quickly into a competitive advantage.
