## Context Usage Patterns

### For Job Description Reviews:
1. **Relevant department role files** (dept_engineering, dept_data_management, or dept_operations)
2. **company_overview.md** (culture alignment and company values)
3. **hiring_guide.md** (interview process and evaluation criteria)
4. **engineering_career_path.md** (if Engineering role - for level expectations)
5. **engineering_overview.md** (if Engineering role - for Engineering team practices)

### For Hiring Process Analysis:
1. **hiring_guide.md** (complete process steps and stages)
2. **column_definitions.md** (data field interpretation)
3. **metrics_definitions.md** (KPI calculations and benchmarks)
4. **Relevant department role files** (for role-specific requirements)

### For Role Comparisons:
1. **Multiple department role files** (compare across or within departments)
2. **engineering_career_path.md** (if Engineering role - progression framework for Engineering roles)
3. **company_overview.md** (consistent culture and value context)
4. **engineering_overview.md** (if Engineering role - for Engineering team practices)

### For Sourcing Strategy Analysis:
1. **hiring_guide.md** (referral program details and sourcing preferences)
2. **metrics_definitions.md** (source effectiveness metrics)
3. **company_overview.md** (culture fit requirements for sourcing)
4. **Relevant department role files** (role-specific sourcing needs)

### For Department-Specific Analysis:
- **Engineering roles**: Use dept_engineering + engineering_career_path.md + engineering_overview.md + company_overview.md
- **Data Management roles**: Use dept_data_management + (optionally dept_engineering) + company_overview.md  
- **Operations roles**: Use dept_operations + company_overview.md
