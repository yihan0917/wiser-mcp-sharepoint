## Wiser's Why (Mission & Vision)
Our customers are primarily retailers (the stores you shop in person or online) or brands (the companies that make the products you buy in those stores). These customers do business today in an increasingly complex and competitive environment. It continues to grow harder for our customers to effectively sell their products to their customers.

At our core, we fundamentally believe we can make that job easier. Our job is to simplify the commerce process (selling products to customers) for our retail and brand customers.

The solutions (see key offerings on Product Strategy) we offer our customers are built to make the commerce process easier. We are the only company in the marketplace that offers the breadth of solutions that help with commerce execution. Each offering is targeted at a particular type of customer or user, but they are meant to work together to help a company drive their sales across multiple channels. We are bringing our different solutions together into a single suite, a one-stop shop. That breadth of offering is a competitive advantage, as a customer can use our different products together to drive improvement in multiple parts of their business. Our products are easy to integrate with our customer’s systems, so they can get the data they need in the systems they work in, at the exact time they need it. We enable our customers to make sense of the large amount of information so they can identify what data points matter so they can quickly take the right actions. That data is high quality so that our customers make the right decisions to drive their business (hence our tagline “better data, better decisions”). We deliver solutions that our customers trust to drive their businesses. We give them a competitive advantage in today’s complex retail environment. We help them win. This is our vision. This is what we build towards every day.

Our ultimate mission is to define a new software segment for retailers and brands called “commerce execution.” The market is consolidating towards this. We want to be there first and we want to be the best. This is our mission. We are bringing the Commerce Execution Suite to life and building ourselves as the market leader. This is how we win.

## How We Win as a Business

Our goal is to build a long term healthy and profitable business that delivers on our mission of defining and leading the commerce execution space.

### Key Business Metrics

- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)** - Money we make from customers each year on renewable annual (or longer) contracts
- **Gross Margin** - The percentage of revenue we keep after direct expenses to run our products (infrastructure, proxies, etc.) have been subtracted
- **Retention** - The percentage of customer revenue that renews with us each year

## Wiser's Product Vision & Strategy
### Product Vision
As described in our Why, our goal as a company is to make commerce execution simpler. We do this by offering solutions across all steps in the value chain for retailers and brands. A value chain is a series of steps that go into the creation of a finished product. In the case of our customers and commerce execution, we are considering the value chain to be the process to sell their merchandise.
Our product vision directly supports our company mission and goals. Much more can be found in the latest Product Strategy.

There are multiple solutions available, which we are building into a suite (and ultimately the fully integrated Commerce Execution Suite).
Wiser Solutions’ Commerce Execution Suite empowers brands and retailers to make decisions, from the physical to the digital shelf.​

Wiser extracts online data so our customers see what their customers see. We surface intelligence at the product category or catalog level, across websites and online marketplaces, so you can monitor competitors, keep a pulse on resellers, and optimize prices. ​​

For in-store needs, smartphone-enabled shoppers provide insights into shopper sentiment, merchandising compliance, and competitive intelligence, and we offer sales teams an end-to-end approach to in-store execution and sales growth. 

### Key Products

- **Price Intelligence** - Catalog price indexing and competitive insights to increase margins and optimize workflows
- **Price Optimization** - An advanced pricing engine, strategy builder, simulation and audit trail to simplify your pricing management
- **Market Intelligence** - Category-wide price and promotions, availability, ratings and reviews, as well as assortment intelligence
- **Live Price Check** - An easy-access browser plugin to track our customer's prices relative to the rest of the market, in real time
- **Digital Shelf Intelligence** - Access to category insights like price, content compliance, search performance, and retail assortment
- **MAP Enforcement** - Build a stronger brand with support for MAP policies and easily communicate with resellers
- **Retail Intelligence** - Plan, measure, and analyze in-store insights, shopper sentiment, and competitive intelligence
- **Retail Execution** - Combine store data with sales insights to improve sales performance and automate workflows

## Commerce Execution Suite
### What is it?
As described on the Product Strategy & Vision page, we offer multiple different products as a “suite.” The current reality is that these products are mostly not integrated with each other.

In order to build the suite, we need an integrated platform underneath it. The platform will give us the necessary shared infrastructure, capabilities and tools to bring our different products together into a single suite. Eventually each of these products will become a module within our customer portal. They will share common components such as authentication, configuration, integration, and reporting. They will bring together our different data sources so the products can work together seamlessly.

What could it look like “under the hood”?
This is a high level view of what that combined platform and suite will be composed of. This is not an architecture diagram, but does provide a preview into the types of capabilities that need to be built, integrated, or merged together to make the platform come to life. This will be a multi-year journey, delivered in increments where we can start realizing value with our customers and migrating them from existing systems. 

What could this look like to our customers? A unified customer experience bringing all our products into one place to work together seamlessly. 

### Why does this matter?
As mentioned in our Why, the suite (and the platform underneath it) is our key to winning. This is the path that unlocks the most comprehensive value for our customers, the best insights, and the easiest integrations. The CES helps us position Wiser as having differentiated value from our many point-solution competitors. It is also the path that gives Wiser a competitive scale advantage. Bringing all the pieces together into a comprehensive whole lets us operate more cost effectively than keeping them separate, and will enable us to use that cost advantage to drive more innovation. 

Our Engineering Strategy is to accelerate the integration and consolidation of our systems so that we can bring that suite to life as quickly as possible, following our Platform & Suite Strategy. This aligns closely to the overall Wiser's Product Vision & Strategy. Tying it back to How We Win as a Business, we have aggressive goals not just to win the market with the suite but to get to profitability in relatively short order. We must consolidate, build the platform of the future and create the suite if we are to achieve all of our company goals.

## Wiser's History

### Overview

Wiser began its journey back in 2013 when the company was founded as Quad Analytix with headquarters in San Mateo, California. Over the past 10+ years, the company has grown to 500+ people across the globe. This has been a mix of both organic growth and acquisition. We have done more acquisitions than the average company. We use acquisitions to strategically expand our market reach into new regions, to add new offerings to our suite more quickly than we could build ourselves, to add technology superior to what we currently have, or to eliminate competition. Acquisition is an integral part of our growth strategy and is baked into our technology vision. We have developed processes for evaluating and integrating new systems and teams into Engineering.
